✏️ Kalimat tentang Warna

What color is it? → Warna apa ini?

It is red. / It's red. → Warnanya merah.

My favorite color is blue. → Warna kesukaanku biru.

The apple is red. → Apel itu berwarna merah.

🎯 Latihan

Coba ucapkan:

The apple is red
The sky is blue
The grass is green
The sun is yellow

✨ Fakta Seru

🌈 Pelangi punya 7 warna: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet.

❤️ Di banyak budaya, merah artinya semangat atau cinta.

💙 Biru sering dikaitkan dengan ketenangan.
