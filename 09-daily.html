🗣️ Kalimat Daily Routine

I wake up at 6 o'clock. → Saya bangun jam 6.

I go to school every day. → Saya pergi sekolah setiap hari.

I play with my friends. → Saya bermain dengan teman.

I sleep at night. → Saya tidur di malam hari.
