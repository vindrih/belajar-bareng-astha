✏️ Apa itu Simple Present?

Simple Present dipakai untuk:

• Kebiasaan sehari-hari

• Fakta umum

Contoh: I eat rice every day.

Rumus: Subject + Verb (dasar)

Untuk he/she/it → tambah -s / -es

📝 Contoh Kalimat

I play football
Saya bermain sepak bola
She reads a book
Dia membaca buku
He goes to school
Dia pergi ke sekolah
We study English
Kami belajar bahasa Inggris
They like apples
Mereka suka apel
The sun rises in the east
Matahari terbit di timur

⚠️ Ingat!

I / You / We / They → verb biasa (play, eat, go)

He / She / It → verb + s/es (plays, eats, goes)

Contoh: She plays | He goes | It rains
